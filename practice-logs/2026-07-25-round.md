# Good–Better–How reflection

## Good

After a shaky warm-up, which left me feeling insecure about my game, I settled myself. Although quite nervous and unsure of my game, I managed to score over the front nine.

My thought process and approach to each shot was consistent.

My general demeanour today was consistent. I kept an even keel. I didn't get too up or down after good or bad shots.

My tee shot today was good with the 3 wood. Plenty of distance.

My chipping around the green was good today too.

I dropped a pre-swing from my game today, and it actually worked well.

## Better

I had many shots inside 100 yards and did not have the confidence to take them on with a pitch. Instead, I played a bump-and-run shot with an 8 iron to get the ball on the green. This often left me with a long putt, which then introduced some 3-putts over the back nine holes.

My 4 hybrid from the fairway. I did not strike the ball well with this club today.

My start line is left, even though my intent is to start the ball right and let it draw left.

I'm pulling my iron shots consistently. I managed to play with the pull, but I did not always get away with it. For example, I ended up in the water on the 18th after a good strike with a pitching wedge.

I lost a shot when I sensed someone was watching me closely; I made a bad swing. This has happened enough times to indicate a pattern. If I sense someone close to me watching me as I prepare to make a swing, it distracts my attention.

## How

I will schedule a technical swing session with video feedback to assess my swing and figure out why I'm pulling the ball and starting the ball left of my intended line, and also why I am not striking it well with the 4 hybrid.

I will schedule block and pressure practice with partial wedge shots so that I build confidence with this shot.

I will continue to work on my fundamentals: setup, grip, ball position and my swing routine.

## Round reflection

### What did I learn?

That even if I have a bad warm-up and my confidence in my game is low, I can still score well and get the ball around the golf course.

### What experience do I want to remember?

That my golf game will never be perfect; there will always be a part of my game that is off on the day. But I can play and score with the game I have, and still enjoy my time on the golf course competing.

### What was the one critical decision I made?

To play the shot or club I have most confidence in at that moment in the game.

### What would I do differently?

To keep swinging freely. When I started to play defensive and safe golf, I dropped more shots.
