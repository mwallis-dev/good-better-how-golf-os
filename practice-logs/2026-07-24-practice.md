# Good–Better–How reflection

## Good

- I started with 10 minutes of lag putting from 20 feet. I was very happy to leave the ball within short putting distance on all putts bar one.
- Around the chipping green, I tried two different techniques and settled on the chip-putt technique, which gave me much better results. This will be the technique I use moving forward for greenside chips.
- With my irons, the low-point drill was very good. I picked the tape up and got the ball on my start line the majority of the time as I moved from wedges into irons.
- Trial and error with the 4 hybrid and 7 wood led to much better success when I used the same setup and feels as my long irons. I will keep this.
- The 3 wood from the tee was also very good, with the ball one position forward and the same swing feels.
- I left with a lot of confidence ahead of tomorrow.

## Better

- My time management: the session ran way over.
- I am getting there with the mini driver, but I did not achieve the same success as with the 3 wood.

## How

- After my round this week, I will get back to experimenting with the mini driver.
- I will continue to work on my setup with the hybrids and woods because I see a lot of potential there.
